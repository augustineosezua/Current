# Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 18, 2026 · Version 2.2

> No account. No bank login. Your budget lives on your phone, and we don't have a copy of it.

## The short version

- **No account. No bank login.** You don't sign up, and Current never connects to your bank.
- **Your budget lives on your phone.** Your balances, transactions, bills, goals and notes are stored on your device. We don't have a copy.
- **AI features run only on your iPhone.** They use Apple's on-device model, and nothing you type or ask is sent to us. On iPhones that don't support it, those features aren't available.
- We collect anonymous usage analytics that never include amounts, merchant names, notes or account names.
- We don't sell your information and we don't show ads.

This policy explains what the developer of Current ("we", "us") handles when you use the app, and why. We follow Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA). Where we offer the app in other countries, we also follow the privacy laws that apply there.

## 1. Data that stays on your device

Everything you enter into Current is stored only in the App's storage on your phone. That includes accounts, balances, transactions, merchants, categories, spending limits, bills, income, goals, term plans, settings and your Ask Current conversations. We can't see it, access it or recover it.

If you import a bank file (CSV, OFX or QFX), the App reads it on your phone and adds the entries to your budget there. The file is never uploaded to us.

That data is included in your iPhone and iCloud device backups if you have them turned on. Those backups are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. CSV exports you create go wherever you choose to save or send them.

## 2. AI features

AI features (quick add, "Can I afford this?", the weekly recap and Ask Current) run on Apple's on-device model, which is part of Apple Intelligence. They work only on iPhones that support Apple Intelligence and have it turned on. The text you type, the questions you ask and the answers stay on your phone. Nothing is sent to us, and we don't use an outside AI service. Apple's handling of Apple Intelligence is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

On iPhones without Apple Intelligence, these features aren't available. Quick add still fills in what it can from your own past entries, on your phone.

If we ever offer AI that runs on a server, it will be optional and off until you agree to it. We'll update this policy and show a notice in the App first.

## 3. Anonymous analytics

To learn which features work, the App sends anonymous usage events to our analytics provider. Events are tied to a random install ID, not to your name, email or device identifiers. Examples include "first transaction logged", "bill added" and "paywall shown". They **never** include amounts, merchant names, notes or account names. We don't record your screen or sessions.

Our analytics provider is PostHog. It stores these events on servers in the United States, so they may be processed outside Canada and be subject to the laws of that country. We have turned off IP-based location and set PostHog not to keep IP addresses; each event carries only your phone's region setting (for example, Canada).

## 4. Purchases

Current Plus is sold through Apple. Apple processes your payment, and we never see your card details. We use RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and purchase receipts from Apple, but no budget data.

## 5. Feedback and surveys

If you tap "Send feedback", your email app opens with a pre-filled message that includes the screen you were on and the App version. We receive it only if you send it. The day-7 survey answers are sent to analytics as free text, and only if you tap send. We keep support emails for up to 3 years after our last reply.

## 6. Notifications

Reminders and summaries are scheduled locally on your phone. We don't send them, and their contents never leave your device.

## 7. Sharing

We don't sell or rent your information. The only third parties involved are the ones named above (Apple, RevenueCat and PostHog), each acting on our behalf, plus anyone we're legally required to disclose information to. Since we don't hold your budget data, we have none of it to share.

## 8. Your rights

You can see, export and delete all of your budget data yourself in the App. Deleting the App deletes that data from your phone, but not from backups you have already made. To ask about the small amount of information we do hold (analytics tied to your install ID, and support emails), or to make an access, correction or deletion request, contact us. We'll reply within 30 days. You can also complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada at priv.gc.ca.

## 9. Children

Current isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from them.

## 10. Changes

If we change this policy in a material way, for example when optional cloud sync arrives for Plus, or if we add optional server-side AI, we'll update the effective date and show a notice in the App before the change takes effect. Cloud sync will always be optional, and this policy will describe it in full before it launches.
